The app couldn't read your cube's Bluetooth address automatically (GAN uses it for encryption). Turn the cube a few times so it's awake and try Connect again first. If it still fails: on your phone, open a free BLE scanner like nRF Connect, find the GAN cube, and read its address. On iPhone, which hides addresses too, the last 6 bytes of its "Manufacturer data" are the address in reverse order. You only need to do this once.
